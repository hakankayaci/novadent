# Home surface brief

- Mode: Persuade. Audience: local and cross-border dental patients researching on mobile.
- Job: establish trust from real clinic evidence and move the visitor to WhatsApp, phone, or directions.
- Proof: supplied clinic photography, exact business details, Google 5.0/141 aggregate, three sourced public excerpts.
- Direction: Luminous Shade Guide. Split-lightbox opening, warm porcelain reading field, deep navy action field, cyan floss route.
- Memorable moment: the NOVA floss line draws once across the split hero and resolves into the treatment path.
- First viewport: compact porcelain header; 43/57 navy-copy/photo split; official logo inside the image; Google proof and two actions on the left.
- Responsive rule: mobile places the photograph first and the navy copy block below it; never overlay long copy on the image.
- Constraints: no stock people, fake social statistics, live Instagram embed, fixed background image, gradient text, or treatment outcome claims.
